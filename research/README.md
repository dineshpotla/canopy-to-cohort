# Research evidence and analysis records

These records document the Michigan recruitment study: its scope, measurement
definitions, comparison plans, source access, alternative explanations, and
numerical checks. They distinguish completed analyses from proposed extensions.

The documentation structure was informed by
[HyperResearch](https://github.com/jordan-gibbs/hyperresearch), reviewed at commit
`15010c5142244b88265f7abadf7b7aa1a8237fde`. Its software harness was not executed
and its performance claims do not validate this study. Review records here
are internal checks, not independent peer review.

The dated reviews and run-status file describe the local analysis before its
public integration. Their publication flags are historical, not a live status
feed. The current public report is available on the
[project website](https://dineshpotla.github.io/canopy-to-cohort/report/recruitment.html).
Unsent correspondence, raw source documents, and record-level observations
are not included.

Publications and local calculations are distinct evidence types. Papers drawing
on overlapping FIA inventories do not count as independent replications of the
Michigan calculations. Source notes record what was actually accessed; an
abstract is not represented as a full-text review. Raw public-source documents
and all record-level FIA observations remain outside the published report.

Search locally with `rg -n 'recruitment|reconcile|sampling' research/notes`.
No external account, new subscription, or research-data upload is required.
