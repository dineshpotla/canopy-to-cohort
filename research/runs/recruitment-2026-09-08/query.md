# Research question and scope

## Primary question

How well do ordinary sugar-maple seedling counts anticipate newly recorded live
saplings at the next FIA inventory in Michigan northern hardwoods, after
accounting for sampled coverage and the interval between visits? Does adding
existing maple sapling density and established-maple basal area improve that
prediction on the same observations?

A companion analysis distinguishes the recorded deaths, continued survival,
and growth of previously tagged saplings. It does not treat disappearance from
a diameter class as synonymous with death.

The implementation is specified in the [analysis contract](analysis-contract.md).
The repeated-visit recruitment endpoint replaces seedling-detection loss as the
main question; the earlier detection analyses remain separate supporting work.

## Scope of the completed Michigan study

The main paper addresses seedling-count information, added maple-stage value,
and linked sapling fates. The [core analysis scope](core-paper-contract.md)
records the required evidence and manuscript checks.

Survey-selection comparisons and regeneration-indicator measurement audits
are secondary. Regional acquisition, added seedling-length prediction, and
deer-exposure modeling are possible follow-up work, not prerequisites for or
findings of the completed core analysis.
