# RI protocol and measurement audit

Continuation declared 2026-09-09, after the survey-priority results and the
record-availability inventory, before calculating height-class associations.
The explored inventory and the known 90-interval/14-event overlap are not an
untouched evaluation sample. This is a local feasibility and measurement audit,
not model development, external validation, or a fieldwork plan.

## Fixed scope

Keep the 922 recruitment-eligible condition intervals and the existing recorded
live-sapling endpoint. Do not relax recruitment eligibility to increase event
support. Baseline RI observations are candidate predictors; follow-up RI
coverage is reported separately and is not required for a baseline predictor
when the existing follow-up TREE sampling frame is valid.

Resolve length/source and sampling-status definitions from official historical
guides and the FIADB dictionary. Record access limitations and any version
changes. Plot-level RI presence alone does not establish a sampled condition.
Require the condition's contributing microplot areas to be covered by valid
baseline RI sampling, linked to the correct subplot and condition records.
Check absent, duplicate, contradictory, invalid-code, and missing-count records.
An absent maple row becomes a conditional tally zero only after these checks;
otherwise the RI measurement remains unavailable. Do not substitute a missing
older status field for the newer microplot status when it is populated.

## Measurement reconciliation

Report six length classes and seedling-source groups using verified code
definitions, without optimizing a grouping against recruitment outcomes.
Prespecify classes 5 and 6 (at least five feet under the published RI schema)
as the descriptive taller-seedling group, motivated by Harris et al. (2022).
Keep the exact class boundaries distinct from FIA's one-inch DBH transition.

Compare ordinary baseline maple counts with RI counts above the ordinary
hardwood minimum length on identical microplot-condition units. Check whether
ordinary counts are derived from RI and whether stump-sprout counting rules
prevent direct equivalence. Present all-size RI counts separately; they must
not be described as interchangeable with ordinary counts. No individual
seedling survival, mortality, or causal browse claim follows from either tally.

## Support and precision gate

Publish sequential eligibility, physical-plot/county support, exclusions,
measurement dates/manuals, valid-zero accounting, and events by declared
height group. Report the baseline-standard-detected subset separately. Use
descriptive uncertainty that respects physical-plot or county clustering when
estimable; avoid stable-looking prediction scores from very few events.

No six-height prediction model will be fitted in this audit. Assess whether
the supported events, predictor distribution, and possible evaluation split
justify planning such a comparison. Illustrative precision calculations must
state their assumptions and are not a power justification for an unspecified
survey decision. A practically meaningful gain and survey costs remain unknown.
If support is weak, retain the measurement audit as the completed contribution
and state the additional evidence needed before forecasting work.

## Outputs and boundaries

Add reproducible R helpers/script, aggregate audit tables, tests, a cited report
section, and revised direction/status notes. Record-level audit data remain
local and Git-ignored. Preserve existing recruitment outputs and unrelated
working-tree changes. Do not acquire other states, infer exact plot locations,
contact field staff, publish, or install a research harness.
