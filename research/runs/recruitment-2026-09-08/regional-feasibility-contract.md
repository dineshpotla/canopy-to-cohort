# Wisconsin–Minnesota feasibility screen

Declared 2026-09-09 after the user authorized downloading and screening these
two public FIA inventories, before inspecting their records. This extends the
completed count-versus-length design; it does not authorize model fitting,
publication, field coordination, or a claim of regional validation.

## Acquisition and provenance

Use official FIA DataMart state SQLite archives. Keep Michigan's pinned source
and existing analyses unchanged. Record exact source URLs, retrieval dates,

integrity checks. Never silently replace an existing source snapshot. Check disk

## Outcome-blinded scientific scope

Keep sugar maple (SPCD 318), baseline sampled northern-hardwood forest types
801, 802, 805, and 809, and an established live maple at least five inches DBH.
Screen RI record coverage, ordinary positive seedling counts, unique repeat
visits, observed 4.5–8.5-year intervals, condition linkage, sampled microplot
frame, baseline RI record validity, and ordinary/RI count reconciliation on the
same area. Follow-up RI is not required for a baseline predictor.

Do not extract follow-up TREE reconciliation codes, sapling-entry counts,
individual tree fates, recruitment labels, or predictor–outcome associations.
Baseline TREE queries are limited to established-maple population membership.
Do not inspect follow-up seedling counts or other ecological outcomes merely to
screen feasibility. A candidate surviving this screen still needs the
predeclared endpoint's outcome-dependent tally checks at evaluation.

Show the flow from all candidate baseline condition-visits to unique physical
plots. For the eventual analysis retain all eligible conditions in one latest
completed compatible interval per physical plot, selected using dates and
protocol/frame criteria, never recruitment outcomes. Use all completed periods
available in each acquired snapshot for this feasibility census, then disclose
their calendar support. Missing dates are not imputed. Ambiguous successors or
condition links are flags, not permission to choose a convenient record.

## Interpretation and next gate

These are regional transport candidates, not independent Michigan validation.
Neither state is assigned to development or evaluation based on results in this
screen. Compare unique-plot support against the previously declared hypothetical
precision scenarios without substituting observed event rates, model scores,
or a new favorable threshold. Small counts do not justify silently widening
forest type, species, geography, follow-up interval, or endpoint.

Retain the earlier annual-manual access qualifications. Database field and
count agreement do not certify every historical field implementation. Leave
event support, model-development adequacy, calibration precision, meaningful
gain, and operational usefulness explicitly unresolved. Before any model work,
freeze source/time/geography roles, endpoint and exclusion code, development
plan, useful-gain criterion, and evaluation/scoring rules.

Deliver reproducible acquisition/screening code, aggregate evidence, tests, and
a plain-language go/no-go assessment with remaining qualifications. Raw
inventories and record-level extracts remain local and git-ignored.
